# D<n> — <one line: the choice, not the problem>

**Decision.** What was chosen, in the present tense, as a reader would need it
restated. One or two sentences.

**Why.** The constraint or the tension that made this the answer. Name what it
costs, not only what it buys.

**Alternatives rejected.** The part a reader six months from now actually needs,
one line each with the reason it lost. A decision with no alternatives is a note,
not a decision — if there genuinely was no choice, say that instead of leaving
the section out.

**Consequences.** What now has to be true, what got harder, and what a future
change to this area has to preserve. Anything a reader could mistake for current
behaviour belongs in the topic page under `docs/`, not here.

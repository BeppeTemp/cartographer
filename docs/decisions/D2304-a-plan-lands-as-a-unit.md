# D2304 — A chain of queued changes lands as one unit, judged by its tip

**Decision.** The lander lands by unit. A queued change's `Lander-After` trailer, or that of the commit a rejected change was queued as (`failed/<slug>^`), links it to the change it names when that one is queued or rejected; changes so linked are one unit, and a change with no link is a unit of one. A unit lands whole, in one train, or not at all: its members in their `Lander-After` order (ties by queue order), the units by their oldest member. A unit is blocked while a member is rejected, held while one carries `Lander-Hold: yes`, and frozen while the freeze is open and one is a `feat`, `perf`, `refactor` or breaking change; otherwise its verdict is the pre-check of its tips, the members no other member follows: red if one is red, else running if one runs, else green. `LAND_MAX` counts changes: a train takes whole green units in order, skipping one that does not fit, and a unit larger than `LAND_MAX` is gated alone; with no green unit, the oldest running unit is gated alone, else the oldest red one. A member rejected at its pick (a conflict, a decision) takes its unit out of the train and alone moves to `failed/`, its reason ending `(chain <first>..<tip>)`; the other members stay queued, blocked until it is queued again. A red train is bisected over the prefixes that end at a unit's boundary; of the first red unit, its last tip in queue order is rejected (`the train gate is red with the chain <first>..<tip> and green without it`), and the green prefix before it lands.

The pre-check a tip carries tests its chain, the changes it follows through `Lander-After`, as the lander would land them (a unit with several tips is tested one path per tip): each job of `ready-check.yml` first replays the change's queued chain (the commit, then the queued change its `Lander-After` trailer names, and so on, each at its queued commit) on the current `main` with a plain cherry-pick, and plans its legs on the union of the queued commits' own diffs. A chain that does not apply is red, including a pick the lander would rescue (an edit of a pending decision `main` holds numbered, a bundle conflict): such a unit is gated alone. A commit that is no longer a `ready/*` tip is red, never tested alone. Queueing a change after another cancels the run still going on that one, which is no longer a tip.

Pending decisions are numbered once per composed train, not at each pick. The picks keep the `D-new-<slug>` placeholders, so a later change that edits a line next to an earlier change's citation, or edits its `D-new-<slug>.md`, applies as written; the one check of a pick is a slug that `main` or an earlier change of the train already holds. After the last pick a citation is checked: a placeholder a change cites must be numbered on `main`, or added by a member of the change's own unit (earlier or later) or by an earlier unit; otherwise that change is rejected with the sentence of an unknown placeholder, its unit is handled as any rejection, and the queue is composed again without a gate. Then the train's pending decisions take the next numbers above the highest on the tree and the newest issue or PR number, in commit order, and each commit is created again in order, same message, author and dates, with its decision files renamed and its citations rewritten in the files the train touched so far (never `_test.go` or `testdata/`); the Atlas bundle is built again for each commit whose own diff adds a citation under `web/` (D2128; also when the pick already rewrote it) and for the later commits that touch `web/` or the bundle, the others carrying the previous commit's, a failed build rejecting that change. `make ready` warns of a citation nothing queued before the change adds.

**Why.** A plan's chain is checked and reviewed once, on its tip (D2040), but the lander read every change's pre-check, took or left each change on its own verdict and bisected change by change. A defect fixed where the review asked, in the plan's last change, left the earlier changes red on their own: they waited or were gated alone, and a bisection could reject one in the middle or land a strict prefix of the plan, whose later changes then had to be rebuilt and queued again. Over two weeks of the queue 45 changes were rejected, 19 of them on red gates. The pre-check tested the queued commit as pushed, on its own diff: a tip's Windows packages were its own, not its chain's, a change queued before a flake was fixed on `main` stayed red until rebuilt, and the runs of changes that were no longer tips competed with the gate for runners. The cost: no change of a chain lands before the whole chain is ready, and one rejected change holds the others.

**Alternatives rejected.**
- Keep landing by change and ask every change of a chain to be green on its own: it puts back the check and review per change that D2040 moved to the tip.
- Test the pre-check on `merge-base(origin/main, HEAD)..HEAD`: a change queued after a predecessor that landed is a child of that predecessor's branch head, whose commits `main` holds squashed, so they would be applied twice.
- Squash a chain into one commit when it is queued: each change stays its own conventional commit on `main`, with its own decision file and changelog entry (D1706).
- Move every member of the unit to `failed/` when one is rejected: the others would have to be queued again for a defect that is not theirs; the one rejected ref already blocks them, and queueing that change again unblocks them.

**Consequences.**
- No train lands a strict prefix of a unit, and after a rejection inside a unit the lander gates nothing of it until the rejected member is queued again: a queued change's `failed/` ref no longer counts (the lander deletes it once the change lands).
- The pre-check of a member that is not a tip is never read; it starts on its push, and is cancelled when a change is queued after it.
- A pre-check verdict is the chain's on the `main` of its run: a re-run after `main` moved tests the new `main` without the change being queued again. A chain that conflicts with `main` is red, so its unit is gated alone; a conflict the lander cannot rescue then rejects it at its pick, before a gate.
- A unit of one is a change as before: its pick, its verdict and its rejection sentences are unchanged.
- A decision is numbered once, so the numbers of a train do not depend on which of its members were rejected before the numbering, and a bisection that lands a prefix keeps the prefix's numbers. A later change's edit of an earlier change's pending decision is an edit of the pending file; the rescue that applies an edit to `D<n>-<slug>.md` stays for a decision whose change landed in an earlier train.
- Amends D1706 (a train takes whole units, and a bisection rejects a unit's tip), D1877 (the verdict is the unit's tips', a red unit is gated alone, and the pre-check tests the queued chain on the current `main`), D1878 (the prefixes of a bisection, probed or gated, end at a unit's boundary) D2040 (a chain checked on its tip lands on that check) and D2128 (the bundle is built again after the one numbering of the train, for the commits that cite a decision under `web/`).

{{- if $s.persistence.emptyDir }}

WARNING: meridian.persistence.emptyDir is on. The data volume is lost with the pod:
  - the audit log (the audit key survives only if the credentials Secret holds it);
  - the conflict registries;
  - usage.json;
  - every KB is cloned again at each restart, so every KB needs a remote, and the
    pushes still pending when the pod is lost are lost too.

# Radio telescopes

Radio waves pass through dust and work by day. They revealed pulsars ([[stars/neutron-star]]) and the [[galaxies/cosmic-microwave-background]]. Linked dishes across the world imaged the shadow of a [[stars/black-hole]] in 2019.

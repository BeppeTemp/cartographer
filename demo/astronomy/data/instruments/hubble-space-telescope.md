# The Hubble Space Telescope

Above the atmosphere, Hubble sees without blurring in visible and ultraviolet light. Its observations of Cepheids ([[stars/variable-stars]]) pinned down the Hubble constant ([[galaxies/hubble-law]]). Its successor in the infrared is the [[instruments/james-webb-space-telescope]].

# The James Webb Space Telescope

JWST observes in the infrared from the Sun–Earth L2 point, 1.5 million km from Earth. Infrared light passes
through dust and carries the light of the earliest galaxies, stretched by [[glossary/redshift]].

Its first images were released on 12 July 2022. It reads the atmospheres of [[stars/exoplanets]] with
[[instruments/spectroscopy]], and its count of bright early galaxies raised a question tracked in
[[questions/early-bright-galaxies]].

# Reflecting telescopes

Newton's design. Mirrors can be made far larger than lenses, which is why every big observatory uses them, from the [[instruments/hubble-space-telescope]] to the [[instruments/james-webb-space-telescope]]. An amateur Newtonian needs its mirrors aligned now and then: [[observing/collimating-a-newtonian]].

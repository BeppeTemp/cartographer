# Telescopes

What matters most is aperture, the diameter of the main lens or mirror: it sets how much light is collected
and how fine the detail can be.

The two classic designs are the [[instruments/refracting-telescope]] and the [[instruments/reflecting-telescope]]; the
[[instruments/radio-telescope]] extends the idea beyond visible light. For a first look at the sky, start with
[[instruments/binoculars]].

# Perseids 2026

## Conditions

New Moon, a dark site at 1,100 m. Followed [[observing/planning-a-meteor-watch]].

## What I saw

Sixty-two meteors in two hours, and one falling star bright enough to cast shadows. The shower's dust comes
from comet Swift–Tuttle ([[solar-system/comets]]).

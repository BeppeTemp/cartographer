# Total solar eclipse of 12 August 2026

## Conditions

Clear sky over the Spanish plateau, the [[solar-system/sun]] low in the west. Partial phases watched through
eclipse glasses as in [[observing/safe-solar-viewing]].

## What I saw

About a minute and a half of totality. The corona spread in long streamers, Venus and Jupiter appeared, and
the temperature dropped noticeably. Contact times are in [contact-times.csv](contact-times.csv).

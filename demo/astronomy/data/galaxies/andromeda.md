# The Andromeda Galaxy

Andromeda (M31) is 2.5 million [[glossary/light-year|light-years]] away and approaching the [[galaxies/milky-way]] at about 110 km/s; the two will merge in a few billion years. In 1923 Edwin Hubble measured a Cepheid in it ([[stars/variable-stars]]) and proved it lies far outside our galaxy.

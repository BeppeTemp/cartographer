# Hubble's law

The farther a galaxy, the larger its [[glossary/redshift]]: the universe is expanding. The ratio is the Hubble
constant, H₀.

Two methods give two values that do not agree: about 67 km/s/Mpc from the
[[galaxies/cosmic-microwave-background]] and about 73 km/s/Mpc from Cepheids and [[stars/supernova|supernovae]].
The disagreement is tracked in [[questions/hubble-tension]].

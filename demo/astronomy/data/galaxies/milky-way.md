# The Milky Way

The Milky Way is about 100,000 [[glossary/light-year|light-years]] across. The [[solar-system/sun]] orbits its
centre once every 230 million years or so. At the centre sits Sagittarius A*, a [[stars/black-hole]] of four
million solar masses.

From a dark site its disc crosses the sky as a band of light: see
[[observing/photographing-the-milky-way]]. It is part of the [[galaxies/local-group]].

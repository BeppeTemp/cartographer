# The cosmic microwave background

Found by accident in 1965 with a horn antenna ([[instruments/radio-telescope]]). Its tiny temperature ripples, mapped by the Planck satellite, fix the age and content of the universe, including [[galaxies/dark-matter]] and [[galaxies/dark-energy]].

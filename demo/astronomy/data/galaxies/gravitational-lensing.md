# Gravitational lensing

Predicted by general relativity and first confirmed during the 1919 solar eclipse. Massive clusters act as natural telescopes, used by the [[instruments/james-webb-space-telescope]] to see very early galaxies; lensing also maps [[galaxies/dark-matter]].

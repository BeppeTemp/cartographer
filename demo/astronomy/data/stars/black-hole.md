# Black holes

Stellar black holes are left by the heaviest stars after a [[stars/supernova]]. Supermassive ones, of millions to billions of solar masses, sit at the centres of galaxies, ours included ([[galaxies/milky-way]]); feeding ones shine as [[galaxies/quasar|quasars]].

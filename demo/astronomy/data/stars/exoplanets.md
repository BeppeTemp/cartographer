# Exoplanets

More than 5,000 exoplanets are confirmed. Most were found by the transit method (a star dims as a planet
crosses it) or by the wobble a planet induces in its star, measured with [[instruments/spectroscopy]].

The [[instruments/james-webb-space-telescope]] now reads the composition of some of their atmospheres. The
nearest is around [[stars/proxima-centauri]].

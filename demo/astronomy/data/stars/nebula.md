# Nebulae

Emission nebulae glow from the light of the young stars inside them; dark nebulae block the light behind them. The gas they hold becomes new stars and planets ([[solar-system/planet-formation]]), and part of it was made inside older stars ([[stars/stellar-nucleosynthesis]]).

# Stars

A star is born in a [[stars/nebula]] when a clump of gas collapses and its core gets hot enough to fuse
hydrogen. It then spends most of its life on the [[stars/main-sequence]].

How it ends depends on its mass: a [[stars/white-dwarf]] for stars like the [[solar-system/sun]], a
[[stars/supernova]] leaving a [[stars/neutron-star]] or a [[stars/black-hole]] for the heaviest ones. The
[[stars/hertzsprung-russell-diagram]] puts the whole story on one chart.

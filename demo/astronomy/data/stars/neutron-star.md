# Neutron stars

What is left after a core-collapse [[stars/supernova]] can be a ball of neutrons about 20 km across. Fast-spinning ones sweep beams of radio waves across the sky: pulsars, found by Jocelyn Bell Burnell with a [[instruments/radio-telescope]] in 1967.

# Supernovae

A supernova can outshine a whole galaxy for weeks. A massive star explodes when its core collapses, leaving a
[[stars/neutron-star]] or a [[stars/black-hole]]; a [[stars/white-dwarf]] explodes when it takes too much mass from a
companion.

The second kind always reaches about the same peak brightness, which makes it a standard candle for measuring
distances to far galaxies ([[galaxies/hubble-law]], [[galaxies/dark-energy]]). Supernovae also spread the
heavy elements made by [[stars/stellar-nucleosynthesis]].

# Stellar nucleosynthesis

Stars fuse hydrogen into helium, and the heavier ones go on to carbon, oxygen and up to iron. Elements heavier than iron come mostly from [[stars/supernova|supernovae]] and neutron-star mergers. The [[solar-system/sun]] is a second- or third-generation star, made of that recycled matter.

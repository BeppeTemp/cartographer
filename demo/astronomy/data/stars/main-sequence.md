# The main sequence

About 90% of stars, the [[solar-system/sun]] included, are on the main sequence. Its band runs across the [[stars/hertzsprung-russell-diagram]] from hot, bright, massive stars to cool, faint, small ones. When the core runs out of hydrogen, the star leaves it and becomes a [[stars/red-giant]].

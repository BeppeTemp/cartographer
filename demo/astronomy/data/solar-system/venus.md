# Venus

Venus is close to [[solar-system/earth]] in size, but its carbon-dioxide atmosphere is ninety times denser and keeps the surface near 465 °C. It rotates backwards, and its day is longer than its year. After the [[solar-system/moon]] it is the brightest natural object in the night sky ([[glossary/apparent-magnitude]] about −4.6).

# Comets

A comet is a nucleus of ice and dust a few kilometres across. Near the [[solar-system/sun]] the ice sublimates into a
coma and two tails, one of dust and one of ions, always pointing away from the Sun.

Short-period comets come from the [[solar-system/kuiper-belt]], long-period ones from the [[solar-system/oort-cloud]]. The dust
they leave along their orbits makes the meteor showers: see [[observing/planning-a-meteor-watch]].
New positions of comets are reported to the [[services/minor-planet-center]].

# Planet formation

Planets form in the protoplanetary disc left over when a star collapses out of a [[stars/nebula]]. Dust
grains stick together into pebbles, pebbles into planetesimals, planetesimals into planets.

Close to the [[solar-system/sun]] only rock and metal survive, which is why the inner planets are rocky
([[solar-system/earth]], [[solar-system/mars]]) and the giants formed farther out. The same process is now observed around other
stars: see [[stars/exoplanets]].

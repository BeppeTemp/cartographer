# The Moon

The Moon is tidally locked to [[solar-system/earth]]: the same face always points at us. It probably formed from the
debris of a giant impact about 4.5 billion years ago ([[solar-system/planet-formation]]).

When it passes through Earth's shadow we see a lunar eclipse, safe to watch with the naked eye: see
[[observing/observing-a-lunar-eclipse]] and the log of [[observations/2026-03-03-total-lunar-eclipse]].

# Pluto

Pluto was discovered in 1930 and counted as the ninth planet for 76 years. In 2006 the IAU defined a planet
as a body that has cleared its orbital neighbourhood, which Pluto has not: it is now a dwarf planet of the
[[solar-system/kuiper-belt]], like [[solar-system/ceres]] in the [[solar-system/asteroid-belt]].

New Horizons flew past it in 2015 and found nitrogen-ice plains and mountains of water ice. The debate over its
status is recorded in [[questions/pluto-planet-status]].

# The Kuiper belt

The Kuiper belt starts beyond [[solar-system/neptune]], 30 to 50 [[glossary/astronomical-unit|AU]] from the Sun. It is home to [[solar-system/pluto]] and the source of many short-period [[solar-system/comets]]. Whether an unseen planet shapes its outer edge is an open question: [[questions/planet-nine]].

# The Sun

The Sun holds 99.8% of the Solar System's mass. It is a [[stars/main-sequence|main-sequence]] star of
spectral type G2V, about 4.6 billion years old, fusing hydrogen into helium in its core
([[stars/stellar-nucleosynthesis]]).

Light leaves its surface and reaches [[solar-system/earth]] about 8 minutes 20 seconds later, across one
[[glossary/astronomical-unit]]. In roughly 5 billion years it will swell into a [[stars/red-giant]] and end
as a [[stars/white-dwarf]].

Never look at it without a certified filter: see [[observing/safe-solar-viewing]].

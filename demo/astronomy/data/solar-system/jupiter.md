# Jupiter

Jupiter has more than twice the mass of all the other planets combined. Its four large moons, first seen by Galileo in 1610, are visible in small [[instruments/binoculars]]; one of them is [[solar-system/europa]]. See the log of [[observations/2026-05-10-jupiter-moons]].

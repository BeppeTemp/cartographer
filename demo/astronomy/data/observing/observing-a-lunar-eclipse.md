# Observing a lunar eclipse

## Goal

Follow the [[solar-system/moon]] through Earth's shadow and record its colour at totality.

## Equipment

- Naked eye; [[instruments/binoculars]] for detail
- A camera on a tripod, optional

## Steps

1. Note the time of each contact from a reliable ephemeris.
2. At the start of the partial phase, watch the dark umbra advance.
3. At totality, rate the Moon's colour on the Danjon scale (0 to 4).
4. Photograph every 5 minutes for a time-lapse.

## Pitfalls

The penumbral phase is almost invisible: do not mistake it for the start of the eclipse.

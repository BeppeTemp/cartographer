# Safe solar viewing

## Goal

Observe the [[solar-system/sun]] or a solar eclipse safely.

## Equipment

- ISO 12312-2 eclipse glasses, or a certified white-light filter on the front of the telescope
- Or a pinhole projector

## Steps

1. Inspect the filter for scratches before every use.
2. Fit the filter on the front of the instrument, never at the eyepiece.
3. Look only through the filter, except during totality.

## Pitfalls

Sunglasses, smoked glass and exposed film are not safe. Only during the total phase of an eclipse can the filter come off: see [[observations/2026-08-12-total-solar-eclipse]].

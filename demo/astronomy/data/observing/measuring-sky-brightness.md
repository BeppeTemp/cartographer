# Measuring sky brightness

## Goal

Know how dark a site is before you plan deep-sky work there.

## Equipment

- A sky-quality meter, or a star chart of a known constellation

## Steps

1. Wait an hour after astronomical twilight.
2. Point the meter at the zenith ([[glossary/zenith]] is not a page yet) and take three readings.
3. Or count the stars visible inside a known constellation and compare with a limiting-[[glossary/apparent-magnitude|magnitude]] chart.

## Pitfalls

The Moon and haze ruin the reading: measure only on clear, moonless nights.

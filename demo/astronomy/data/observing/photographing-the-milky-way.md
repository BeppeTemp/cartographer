# Photographing the Milky Way

## Goal

Capture the core of the [[galaxies/milky-way]] over a foreground with a fixed camera.

## Equipment

- A camera with manual mode and a fast wide-angle lens
- A sturdy tripod
- Star charts in `{{path:star-charts}}`

## Steps

1. Pick a moonless night at a dark site ([[observing/measuring-sky-brightness]]).
2. Focus manually on a bright star with live view at maximum zoom.
3. Expose at f/2.8, ISO 3200, for at most 500 divided by the focal length, in seconds.
4. Upload the raw frames to the [[services/observatory-archive]], then stack them to reduce noise.

## Pitfalls

Longer exposures trail the stars unless the camera sits on a tracker with good [[observing/polar-alignment]].

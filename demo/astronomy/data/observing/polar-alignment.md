# Polar alignment

## Goal

Point the mount's polar axis at the celestial pole, so one motor can follow the sky's rotation.

## Equipment

- An equatorial mount with a polar scope
- A phone app or chart showing where [[stars/polaris]] sits around the pole

## Steps

1. Level the tripod.
2. Set the latitude scale to your latitude.
3. Point the polar axis roughly north.
4. In the polar scope, place [[stars/polaris]] at the position your app shows on the reticle.
5. Lock the azimuth and altitude bolts.

## Pitfalls

Polaris is not exactly on the pole: putting it in the centre of the scope leaves an error of almost a degree, enough to trail long exposures ([[observing/photographing-the-milky-way]]).

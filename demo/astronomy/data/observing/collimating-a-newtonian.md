# Collimating a Newtonian

## Goal

Bring the optical axes of the primary and secondary mirrors into line, so stars focus to a point.

## Equipment

- A collimation cap or a laser collimator
- A screwdriver for the secondary holder

## Steps

1. Point the tube horizontally.
2. Centre the secondary under the focuser.
3. Tilt the secondary until the primary is centred in it.
4. Tilt the primary until its centre mark is centred.
5. Check on a star at high power.

## Pitfalls

A laser that is not itself collimated will lie. Check it by rotating it in the focuser.

# Planning a meteor watch

## Goal

Count meteors from a shower near its peak.

## Equipment

- A reclining chair and warm clothes
- Nothing optical: meteors are a naked-eye show

## Steps

1. Find the peak date and the radiant of the shower ([[solar-system/comets]] leave the dust).
2. Check the Moon's phase: a bright Moon hides the faint meteors.
3. Let your eyes adapt for 20 minutes.
4. Look about 45° away from the radiant and count meteors per hour.

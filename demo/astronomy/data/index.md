# Astronomy atlas

A small atlas of the sky, from the Solar System to the edge of the observable universe, with the procedures
and the observation log of an amateur observatory. It is the demo knowledge base of Cartographer.

- [Solar System](solar-system/index.md) — The Sun, its planets, their moons and the small bodies around them.
- [Stars](stars/index.md) — How stars are born, live and die, and the notable ones in our sky.
- [Galaxies and Cosmology](galaxies/index.md) — Galaxies, the large-scale universe and its history.
- [Instruments](instruments/index.md) — Telescopes, space observatories and the techniques they enable.
- [Observing](observing/index.md) — Procedures for a night under the sky, from setup to photography.
- [Glossary](glossary/index.md) — The terms this atlas uses, defined once.
- [Observation Log](observations/index.md) — Dated notes from observing sessions.
- [Sources](sources/index.md) — The source ledger: papers, datasets and pages this atlas was built from.
- [Open Questions](questions/index.md) — Where the sources disagree, and what the atlas does not know yet.

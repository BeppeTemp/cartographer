# Is there a ninth planet?

Some distant objects of the [[solar-system/kuiper-belt]] have clustered orbits, and
[[sources/2016-01-20-batygin-brown-planet-nine]] proposes an unseen planet to explain them. Others attribute
the clustering to observational bias. No survey has found the planet yet.

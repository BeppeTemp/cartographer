# Too many bright early galaxies?

The [[instruments/james-webb-space-telescope]] found galaxies at very high [[glossary/redshift]] that look
brighter and more numerous than galaxy-formation models predicted. Whether the models, the distance estimates
or the brightness estimates need revising is still debated.

# The Hubble tension

[[galaxies/hubble-law]] cites two values of H₀ that are both precise and do not overlap:

- 67.4 ± 0.5 km/s/Mpc from the cosmic microwave background
  ([[sources/2018-07-17-planck-2018-cosmological-parameters]]);
- 73.0 ± 1.0 km/s/Mpc from Cepheids and supernovae ([[sources/2021-12-08-sh0es-hubble-constant]]).

Either one of the methods has a systematic error nobody has found, or the standard cosmological model is
missing something. Until it is settled, pages should quote both values.
